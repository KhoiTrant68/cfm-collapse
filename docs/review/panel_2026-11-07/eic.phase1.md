## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor, mandatory, owned by the methodology seat). From an editorial-oversight standpoint, this dimension asks whether the design of the work, its data handling, the way it reports quantitative results, and what it gives readers to reproduce those results would satisfy the normal peer-review standard of the field. It is not mine to score; I note only that the panel's decision depends on it and that a fatal block here would end the submission on its own.

D2 (domain_accuracy, mandatory, owned by the domain seat). This dimension asks whether the claims match the current state of evidence in the field, whether earlier work is described faithfully, and whether field-specific terms and results are stated correctly. It is not mine to score, but as the journal-fit seat I rely on its outcome when judging whether any claimed novelty over prior work is real.

D3 (argumentative_coherence, mandatory, owned by the devil's-advocate seat with the methodology seat also eligible). This dimension asks whether the central thesis holds together, whether the evidence offered actually carries the claims made, and whether any reasoning error undermines the main argument. It is not mine to score; editorially, it is the check that what the headline promises is what the body delivers.

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance, high, owned by the perspective seat). This dimension asks whether framing, definitions, and implications can be followed by readers from neighbouring fields, and whether any claim that reaches across disciplines is backed up. It is not mine to score; a block here would by itself push the panel toward major revision.

D5 (writing_and_structure, normal, owned by me). This dimension asks whether the manuscript is organised so a reader can follow it, whether the exposition is clear, whether figures and tables do their job, and whether the submission follows the conventions of the configured venue, including its page budget and how much the main text leans on the appendix. It is the lowest-priority dimension and cannot be fatal, but any warn or block here still moves the panel off an accept outcome.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution, mandatory, owned by me). This dimension asks whether the manuscript belongs at the configured venue and whether it offers something original and significant for that venue's readers. It is the core of my seat: I judge whether the stated contribution is new relative to the work the submission positions itself against, whether it matters to the venue's audience, and whether the headline can be stated and checked within the main text. Because it is mandatory, a block here forces at least major revision and a fatal block forces rejection.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure
dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: Whether the main text alone states the setting, the headline results, and their scope clearly within the venue page limit; whether section order follows the argument; whether figures and tables are legible, captioned, and referenced; whether notation is introduced before use and stays consistent; and whether the main text can be read without constant jumps to the appendix.
what_triggers_block: The main text cannot be followed on its own: core definitions, theorem statements, or the experimental setup needed to understand the headline are missing from it or appear only in the appendix, or the submission breaks a hard venue formatting or length convention.
what_triggers_warn: The main text is followable but needs repair: unclear or inconsistent notation, figures or tables that are hard to read or not tied to the text, frequent but non-essential appendix dependence, or organisation that hides the headline behind technical preliminaries.

### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution
dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: Whether the contribution is stated precisely and early; whether it is clearly separated from the closest prior memorisation, generalisation, and guidance results the submission cites, with a statement-level comparison where overlap is likely; whether readers at the configured venue gain a new, checkable insight that bears on practical samplers rather than only on an idealised object; and whether the scope and limits of each claim, including any negative results, are stated honestly.
what_triggers_block: The claimed novelty is substantially overstated: a main result is equivalent to or directly implied by a cited or well-known prior result without that being acknowledged, or the link from the idealised analysis to anything the venue's readers would use is asserted but not shown.
what_triggers_warn: The contribution is real but its positioning needs work: the delta over the closest prior work is stated vaguely or only in the appendix, significance for practical samplers is argued thinly, or the framing promises more generality than the stated results support.
what_triggers_fatal: The submission has no original contribution for the configured venue: every main result is already established in prior work, or the work falls outside the venue's scope so that no revision of framing or positioning could make it fit.
