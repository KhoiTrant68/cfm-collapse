## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor, mandatory, owned by methodology): From an adversarial standpoint, this dimension asks whether the way the work establishes its results could survive a hostile replication attempt. I would probe whether the design, the data handling, and the reporting leave room for an outcome to be an artefact of setup choices, whether reported quantities come with enough information to judge variability, and whether a skeptical reader could rerun or re-derive what is claimed. I do not score this dimension, but any weakness I find here feeds my challenges.

D2 (domain_accuracy, mandatory, owned by domain): This dimension asks whether what the work says about its field, its prior work, and its terminology is correct and current. As devil's advocate I would look for places where prior results are framed so as to make the contribution look larger, where established terms are used in a non-standard sense without warning, or where a claimed gap in the literature is not actually a gap. I do not score this dimension.

D3 (argumentative_coherence, mandatory, owned by da): This is the dimension I own. It asks whether the central thesis holds together from premises to conclusions: whether every headline claim is actually carried by the evidence or derivations offered, whether the stated scope of each claim matches the scope of what supports it, whether the chain of reasoning has hidden steps, circularity, or equivocation between related notions, and whether the strongest alternative explanation of the same evidence has been ruled out rather than ignored. A coherent argument is one where an adversary cannot pull out a single link and watch the conclusion fall.

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance, high, owned by perspective): This dimension asks whether readers from adjacent areas can follow the framing and definitions and whether any claims about implications beyond the core area are substantiated rather than asserted. My adversarial interest is in implication claims that outrun what was shown. I do not score this dimension.

D5 (writing_and_structure, normal, owned by eic): This dimension asks whether the organisation, exposition, figures, and tables make the work easy to verify and follow, and whether venue conventions are respected. My adversarial interest is in presentation that obscures where a claim is actually supported or that buries caveats. I do not score this dimension.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution, mandatory, owned by eic): This dimension asks whether the work belongs at the configured venue and whether its contribution is original and significant for that readership. My adversarial interest is the "so what?" test: whether the contribution would survive if a reviewer argued it is a restatement, a special case, or an expected consequence of known results. I do not score this dimension.

## Scoring Plan

### D3: argumentative_coherence
dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: For each headline claim, trace the exact premises, assumptions, and derivation or evidence that carry it; check that stated scope matches supported scope, that terms keep one meaning throughout, that no step silently relies on an unstated assumption, and that the strongest rival explanation of the same evidence is addressed.
what_triggers_block: A headline claim is stated more broadly than its supporting argument establishes (an unhedged overgeneralisation, a missing case, or a load-bearing assumption left unstated), so the claim as written is unsupported although a narrower, correctly scoped version would plausibly stand after revision.
what_triggers_warn: The core argument holds, but there are local gaps such as an equivocation between closely related notions, a skipped but repairable step, an unaddressed but non-fatal alternative explanation, or caveats that are present but placed where a reader would miss them.
what_triggers_fatal: The central thesis is contradicted by the paper's own premises or evidence, rests on a circular or invalid inference at a load-bearing step, or depends on an assumption under which the main claim becomes vacuous, so that no scope reduction leaves a meaningful contribution.
