\subsection{Which image is decided early, whether it is reached is decided late}
\label{sec:edm-clocks}

We test the late-error picture on class-conditional EDM models on CIFAR-10 \citep{karras2022elucidating}:
the public model trained on all $50{,}000$ images and a model trained here on a class-balanced
subset (TODO: sub500 numbers). In EDM coordinates $x$ at noise level $\sigma$ the paper's
interpolant is $Z=tx$ with $t=1/(1+\sigma)$, the data prediction is the denoiser,
$\hat x(Z,t)=D(Z/t,\sigma)$, and the one-sided Lipschitz constant of
Lemma~\ref{lem:contraction} % TODO label of Claim 1 once added
is $\mu=(1+\sigma)\lambda_{\max}(\operatorname{sym}\partial_x D)$. The exact field is the class
posterior mean over the training images. All samples use EDM's deterministic 18-step Heun sampler
and the off-atom criterion $d_1/d_2\ge 1/3$ of App.~\ref{app:measured-transfer}.

\paragraph{Swapping the field.}
From the same latents we run four samplers: the network throughout, the exact field throughout,
the network for $\sigma>s$ and the exact field below (\emph{net$\to$exact}), and the reverse
(\emph{exact$\to$net}). On the public model every network sample ends off-atom and every exact
sample on-atom. Net$\to$exact ends on-atom for every $s$, including $s=0.02$ ($t=0.98$): replacing
the field on the last $2\%$ of the time turns $0\%$ memorization into $100\%$. The image it lands on
is the network trajectory's nearest training image in $31\%$, $56\%$, $90\%$, $99\%$ and $100\%$ of
samples for $s=5,2,1,0.2,0.02$ (sub1000 model: $49\%,63\%,88\%,98\%,100\%$). Exact$\to$net ends
off-atom for $s\ge 1$ and on-atom for $s\le 0.1$, but its endpoint distance to the image does not
shrink after the hand-over: the median $d_1$ is $2.6$, $1.4$ and $0.6$ for $s=0.1,0.05,0.02$,
roughly proportional to $s$.

\paragraph{Two clocks.}
The swap separates two times (Prop.~\ref{prop:twoclocks}). % TODO label (Prop. T)
The \emph{selection clock} $t_{\rm sel}$ is the time after which the network trajectory stays in the
exact field's basin of one image. The net$\to$exact agreement curve upper-bounds its distribution
function (the difference is the mass of trajectories that leave their final basin and return), so
selection happens no earlier than the curve suggests: at most $31\%$ of samples have selected by
$\sigma=5$, $56\%$ by $\sigma=2$ and $90\%$ by $\sigma=1$. Its limit of $100\%$ as $s\to0$ carries
no information: the basins tend to Voronoi cells, so it holds for any learned flow whose endpoints
avoid Voronoi ties. This is compatible with the medium-noise band
where \citet{dodson2026} % TODO-bib
locate memorization. The \emph{landing} is whether the trajectory contracts onto that image, and
it is set by the late Jacobian. Under the local contraction condition $\mu\le\bar\mu<1$ on a tube
around the exact path started from the hand-over point, the trajectory ends within
$\eta/(1-\bar\mu)$ of the image (Prop.~\ref{prop:localcert}). % TODO label (Prop. L)
Conversely, when $\lambda_{\min}(\operatorname{sym}\partial_x D)\approx1$ late, the distance to the
image can shrink only through the network's residual $D(x^i,\sigma)-x^i$ at that image
(Prop.~\ref{prop:twoclocks}(ii)). % TODO label (Prop. T)
The exact field ($\mu^*=0$) is what makes the landing visible; that a network$\to$network swap at
small $\sigma$ leaves memorization unchanged \citep{dodson2026} is consistent with this but is not
implied by it.

\paragraph{Why a trained EDM network does not land.}
Preconditioned networks write $D=c_{\rm skip}x+c_{\rm out}F(c_{\rm in}x)$ with $c_{\rm skip}\to1$ and
$c_{\rm out}c_{\rm in}=O(\sigma)$, so $\partial_x D\to I$ unless the raw network's Jacobian blows up
as $\sigma\to0$. Prop.~\ref{prop:param} % TODO label (Prop. P)
makes this precise: if $1-\lambda_{\min}(\operatorname{sym}\partial_x D)\le\bar\rho$ on
$[\sigma_a,\sigma_b]$, distances between trajectories shrink by at most
$(\sigma_a/\sigma_b)^{\bar\rho}$, and if $\sup_x\|\operatorname{sym}\partial F\|$ is integrable at
$\sigma=0$ the flow map is bi-Lipschitz, so no trajectory ends exactly on a training image and no
two trajectories merge. Integrability fails for the exact field, whose raw Jacobian is at least
$\sigma_d/\sigma$. The condition is necessary, not sufficient, and applies to any concentration on a
null set, not only to memorization. Measured
along the network trajectories, the median $\mu$ is $4.9$, $2.7$, $1.7$, $2.1$ and $1.00$ at
$t=0.34$, $0.63$, $0.88$, $0.98$ and $0.998$ on the public model, never below $1$ before $t=0.98$,
while the exact field has $\mu^*=0$ from $t\ge0.63$; on the sub1000 model the share is
$\rho=1-\lambda_{\max}(\operatorname{sym}\partial_x D)/c_{\rm skip}\approx0.015$ for
$\sigma\le0.023$, so the sufficient condition for landing fails: it allows at most a $6\%$
contraction over the remaining schedule. TODO (sub500): $\mu$ and $\rho$ for on-atom against off-atom samples, AUC, and comparison
with the nearest-image baseline.
